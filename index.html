Punto

A padel scoreboard you stand facing the court, with a coach on demand. It keeps the score with star point, golden point or advantage, shows who serves from which side and who receives, times every point, game and set, listens for ball bounces, and saves every match to your history.

  1. New match: enter names, positions (drive / revés), serve order and the deuce rule.
  2. After each point, tap the half of the team that won it. A high beep means your team got the point, a low beep the rivals, a long deep beep an undo. Undo is always one tap away.
  3. After every game a screen shows who serves to whom. Tap Coach whenever you want one focus tip and an optional "what hurt" tag.
  4. Finished matches are saved automatically; press End to save an unfinished one when your court time runs out.
